Bortezomib clears it.

Aneuploid cells carry a protein imbalance, so they lean on the proteasome. In myeloma patients, higher aneuploidy tracked with response to proteasome inhibitors. But that is cancer aneuploidy, not constitutional — our experiment is built to break that transfer.

What changes his prognosis today is not a molecule.

Bortezomib fails filter four for him: motor neuropathy in 8% of children, on top of existing muscle atrophy. The realistic threat is a second embryonal tumour — so the intervention that matters now is surveillance.

What this is not

Not a prescription.

The proteasome dependency is shown in cancer aneuploidy. Whether it transfers to constitutional MVA is unproven — which is exactly what the experiment tests.

What this is

A prepared,
testable answer.

A tumour-board hypothesis with a concentration margin, a stopping rule, and an eight-week experiment that could kill it.

The five-filter framework is the part that scales — it applies to any rare disease where someone is about to propose an approved drug.
