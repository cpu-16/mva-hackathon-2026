Absorption

Trametinib is absorbed orally with median time to achieve peak concentrations of 1.5 hours post-dose.
The mean absolute bioavailability of a single 2 mg tablet dose is 72% relative to an intravenous (IV)
microdose. The increase in exposure (Cmax and AUC) was dose-proportional following repeat dosing.
Following administration of 2 mg once daily, steady-state geometric mean Cmax, AUC(0-) and predose
concentration were 22.2 ng/ml, 370 ng*hr/ml and 12.1 ng/ml, respectively with a low peak:trough
ratio (1.8). Inter-subject variability at steady state was low (<28%).

Trametinib accumulates with repeat daily dosing with a mean accumulation ratio of 6.0 at 2 mg once
daily dose. Steady state was achieved by Day 15.

Administration of a single dose of trametinib with a high-fat, high-calorie meal resulted in a 70% and
10% decrease in Cmax and AUC, respectively compared to fasted conditions (see sections 4.2 and 4.5).
