## Verdict

These three texts do not contain one decision. The family page tells the clinic not to start a drug, then says a future tumour makes bortezomib worth a tumour board and that the answer is already written; the executive summary says an active malignancy still would not establish a favourable benefit–risk balance; the methods abstract says the drug passes the first two filters and fails the fourth on motor neuropathy in 8%, while Part I fails it on peripheral neuropathy in 18% (25/140) and changes the child’s finding from muscle weakness to muscle atrophy. Filter 3 is defined as whether the achievable concentration reaches the effective concentration, then scored “not excluded” with a 5.8–7.8× multiple of “the EC50” that the next sentence reclassifies as a non-margin against a 40 nM reference, with free peak about 1.0–1.3× that reference. The fifth filter is advertised and never scored, the myeloma evidence called motivation is also asked to carry a pass, and “transfers to any rare disease” is supported only by MVA2 and MVA3. The surveillance items are the part a clinic could use, and even there the orbital action and the miscarriage sentence are stronger than the single case and the single study printed beside them. I would not accept this as the decision document until each filter status, the neuropathy numerator, the phenotype, and the tumour-board sentence are the same sentence on every page.
